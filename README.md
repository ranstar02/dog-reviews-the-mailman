A one-line review a dog gives the mailman — five stars for the easiest protection racket on the street. The program writes one funny review (160 characters, a single line) to `/output/entry.txt`: the mailman shows up daily, lets the dog bark him off the porch, then pays him in paper to shred — and business is booming.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It validates the review (1–200 characters, one line, no leading/trailing or double spaces), writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (falling back to `output/entry.txt`), reads it back and asserts it matches exactly.

## Entry

> Five stars. He shows up daily, lets me bark him off my porch, then pays me in paper to shred — easiest protection racket on the street, and business is booming.
